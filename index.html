33 → 25 minP99 node recovery time
  • Reliability
  • Incident automation

Read case study +

The challenge

Communication failures between nodes and the cluster-level fabric controller affected recovery time. Many incidents also lacked attribution to a responsible team.

My contribution

Worked on improving node recovery for these communication failures and added automation to route incident-management tickets to the right teams.

The result

Reduced P99 recovery time from 33 to 25 minutes, approximately a 24% improvement. Reduced unattributed communication-failure incidents from 70% to 33% of weekly cases.

The attribution percentages describe weekly incident populations, not a one-week implementation timeline.
